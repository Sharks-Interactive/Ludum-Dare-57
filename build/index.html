meaning.

You know the irritating feeling when you know what a word means, but can't explain it?
Get ready to experience that several times over...

what to expect:

Once you hit start, you will be given a word and then have 30 seconds to come up with as many synonyms for it as possible - in other words, you have to dig deeper into the true meaning of the word. After the 30 seconds is up, you'll move onto the next word, 5 total.
